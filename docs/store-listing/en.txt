Customize how you watch Netflix with up to three shortcuts per action.

Shortcuts and playback

- Keep your regular episode shortcuts and add your keyboard’s Previous Track and Next Track keys when your browser supports them.
- Play or pause, step through frames, switch episodes, adjust volume and speed, and cycle through subtitle sizes, subtitle languages, or audio tracks.
- Create custom forward and rewind shortcuts from 1 to 300 seconds, jump to a percentage of the video, or set a preferred playback speed up to 5×. Hold your Play / Pause key to temporarily use your chosen hold speed.

Subtitles and Picture-in-Picture

- Turn on subtitle navigation to jump between subtitles, replay the current one, or use a separate Play / Pause shortcut. Select a Netflix subtitle track and grant subtitle website access to enable it.
- Watch in Picture-in-Picture with Netflix subtitles and playback controls on Chrome and Edge 116 or later.

Playback preferences and backups

- Optionally skip intros or recaps, play the next episode automatically, or hide Netflix’s pause overlay.
- Sync your settings, export and import backups, and find help in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, or Indonesian.

Shortcuts work on Netflix playback pages and are skipped while you type in editable fields. Some features depend on Netflix’s player and may need updates when it changes.

Privacy

No analytics, tracking, or third-party processing services. Settings use browser sync storage.

Optional subtitle navigation retrieves subtitles from Netflix delivery hosts and processes them locally, with timing data kept temporarily in memory.
