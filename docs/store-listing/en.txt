Customize Netflix playback shortcuts to suit the way you watch.

- Customize shortcuts for play/pause, seeking, volume, mute, Netflix subtitles, fullscreen, skip intro, and playback speed.
- Hold your configured Play / Pause key to temporarily use your selected hold speed. Release it to restore the previous speed and playback state.
- Adjust seek intervals, speed steps, and preferred playback speed.
- Enable optional subtitle navigation to jump to the previous or next subtitle, replay the current subtitle, and toggle playback with customizable keys. Select a Netflix subtitle track and grant subtitle website access to use this feature.
- Watch in Picture-in-Picture with Netflix subtitles and playback controls on Chrome and Edge. This feature is unavailable on Firefox.
- Sync settings through your browser, export/import backups, and choose English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, or Korean.

Shortcuts run on Netflix playback pages and are skipped while you type in editable fields. Some features depend on Netflix player interfaces and may need updates when Netflix changes them.

Privacy: No analytics, tracking, or third-party processing services. Settings use browser sync storage. Subtitle navigation retrieves the selected subtitles from Netflix delivery hosts and processes them locally; the timing cache stays temporarily in memory.

This is an unofficial extension, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Netflix.
